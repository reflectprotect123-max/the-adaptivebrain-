# Recommended additions and categorized inventory

## Bottom line

The system is already coherent. The highest-value additions are clearer slots and decision rules, not more exercise variety.

## Strength

| Item | Priority | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| True hinge slot | P0 | Rotate in an RDL, trap-bar deadlift, or deadlift variation after the current block. This fills the lengthened hip-hinge gap without simply adding volume. |
| Power primer | P0 | Formalize the existing Monday box-jump and Thursday plyometric-push-up primers: 3 x 3–5, fresh, crisp, stop before fatigue. |
| Trunk slot | P1 | Keep carries, and rotate in ab-wheel, side plank, Pallof press, or suitcase carry for 2–3 controlled sets. Replace optional arm volume rather than adding work. |
| Carries and sleds | Keep | Retain farmer carries/backward sled drags as useful low-skill work-capacity and trunk work. |
| Main-lift progression | Keep | Hold the main lifts stable; progress only when reps and execution are achieved, using the smallest load jump. |

## Conditioning

| Item | Priority | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Two lanes | P0 | Easy Mono at RPE 3–4 plus Moderate Mixed at RPE 5–6; keep them separate from lifting by default. |
| Return progression | P0 | 12 x 15s/45s → 15 x 15s/45s → 2 x 5 min → 3 x 5 min → 4 x 5 min. Hard means controlled/moderately hard, not sprinting. |
| Easy aerobic lane | P0 | Build a conversational run/walk toward 20–40 minutes. Increase one lever at a time: duration, repetitions, intensity, recovery, or pace/power. |
| Quality gate | P0 | If cardiovascular effort passes but mechanics or joint comfort deteriorate, repeat or reduce the dose. |
| Modality rules | P1 | Do not assume run, row, and bike minutes are interchangeable; preserve the intended modality and stress. |

## Recovery and readiness

| Item | Priority | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Work-load capture | P0 | Log work hours, parcels/carrying, steps, sleep, stress, soreness, pain, illness, and energy beside training. |
| Decision states | P0 | Use: progress, repeat, hold, regress, substitute, reschedule, maintain, taper, stop/refer, or no-data. |
| Signal hierarchy | P0 | Safety/pain/illness first; then warm-up and task performance, athlete report, recent exposure, and wearable trends as context. No HRV/body-battery-only decisions. |
| Re-entry rule | P0 | After interruption, hold the last successful dose, reduce sets/duration if needed, preserve the intent, and do not stack make-up sessions. |
| Christmas mode | P0 | Late November–Christmas: two 35–45 minute strength sessions, main lifts 2–3 x 3–6 at 2–4 RIR, accessories about 2 sets, optional 15–25 minute easy conditioning; no PR chasing. |

## Testing and monitoring

| Item | Priority | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Submax baseline | P0 | Record estimated front-squat/floor-press strength, chin-up performance, broad jump, 20–30 minute run/walk distance, 2-minute heart-rate recovery, RPE, and leg/breathing comfort. |
| Retest | P1 | Retest after a complete 4–8 week exposure, not immediately after returning from a layoff. |
| Weekly review | P0 | Compare planned versus completed training, not just wearable scores. |

## TrainHeroic database

The OG database is included in the archive under `08-original-research/trainheroic/` and should be treated as a historical/programming source.

Add or preserve these fields in the working database:

- scheduled/template-only vs actually logged/modified/missed;
- session order: warm-up, primer, main lift, secondary work, conditioning, cooldown;
- movement pattern, intent, slot, equipment, fatigue cost, substitutions, constraints, aliases;
- planned versus actual sets, reps, load, RIR/RPE, duration, modifications, and reason;
- last successful load/reps and the next permissible progression.

Do not auto-dump every historical session into the current plan. Curate from the database into the stable slots above.

## Research and design rules

- Label claims as observed, research-supported, conditional, inferred, product heuristic, or unknown.
- Keep concurrent-training interference in the scheduling rules: hard running needs more protection around lower-body strength than easy row/bike work.
- Separate testing from training and preserve uncertainty in the data.
- Keep the 4-session chassis: two non-consecutive full-body strength days and up to two separate conditioning days.
- Minimum viable week: 2 strength. Base week: 2 strength + 1 conditioning. Full week: 2 strength + 2 conditioning.

## Deliberately not adding

No fifth training day, random HIIT, AMRAP/for-time conditioning, universal run↔row↔bike conversions, arbitrary zone-minute targets, acute:chronic workload-ratio rules, or more exercise variety for its own sake.

## Recommended next block

Keep the current template unchanged except for: one rotating true hinge, the formal power primers, one trunk slot (usually by replacing some arm volume), and the submax baseline. Everything else is a progression and review layer.
