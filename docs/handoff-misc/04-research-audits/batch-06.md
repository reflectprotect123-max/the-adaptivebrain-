# The Everyday Responder research — Batch 06

## Scope and method

Assigned range: Episodes 42–49.

Metadata was located in the official Buzzsprout RSS feed and checked against each official Buzzsprout episode page. The episode pages expose show notes and audio metadata; none of these eight pages exposed a public transcript panel, and the Chapters tab was hidden on each page checked. No full transcripts are reproduced here. The summaries below are original, conservative research notes based on the published show notes.

- **Key notes:**

## Episode 44

- **Title:** The Counterintuitive Approach to Running Faster for Tactical Athletes
- **Date:** 2025-10-03
- **Duration:** 33:11
- **Official URL:** https://theeverydayresponder.buzzsprout.com/2427372/episodes/17952982-episode-44-the-counterintuitive-approach-to-running-faster-for-tactical-athletes
- **Transcript status:** Show notes only; no public transcript or chapters visible.
- **Timestamps:** None published.
- **Research summary:** The episode presents five counterintuitive strategies for improving run times. The published notes point to establishing the right baseline before starting, focusing on a variable other than pace or distance, questioning overreliance on a watch, using faster running to learn easier running, and recognizing when coaching may be useful. The notes frame the approach as disciplined execution rather than a shortcut.
- **Key notes:**
  - Baseline testing should precede an automatic increase in running volume or speed.
  - Pace and distance are not presented as the only useful training variables.

- **Tags:** running, run-times, baseline-testing, pacing, coaching, endurance, ego-management
- **Hybrid relevance:** Directly relevant to the conditioning slot. Preserve the selected running modality, start from a simple baseline, and progress one clear variable at a time; do not add multiple run tests or watch metrics to every session.
