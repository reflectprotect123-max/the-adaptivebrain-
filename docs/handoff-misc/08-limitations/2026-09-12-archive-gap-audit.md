# Archive Gap Audit — 12 September 2026

## Confirmed problem

The previous ZIP overrepresented conditioning. It contained eight dedicated conditioning files, four recovery/readiness files and fifteen general research audits, but no dedicated strength directory. Strength work was buried inside the large system dossier and one Peak Strength/Garage Strength audit, making it appear much shallower than the work completed.

## Added in this correction

- Dedicated strength-system consolidation.
- Full progression/recovery/regression research synthesis relevant to strength.
- The 120-exercise library in Markdown, CSV and JSON forms with its README.
- Product and technical design document.
- Coaching-platform market research covering notation, prescription, progression, trust UX and anti-patterns.
- TrainHeroic source-data inventory.

## Deliberately excluded

- Personal strength splits and personal RHR examples.
- Nutrition-project material.
- Duplicate files and duplicate rendered formats that add no information.
- Old HTML prototypes that demonstrate screens but add no unique researched evidence.
- Stale strength-block instructions that prohibit automatic load suggestions or specify an old three-day block.
- The old Coach Brain and recovery-design files that require pain/symptom check-ins, because pain is not a current strength or conditioning input.
- The large uncompressed Aerobic 40 duplicate; the compact reference already exists.

## Remaining limitations

This archive can include recovered files and current Library artifacts, but it cannot guarantee verbatim recovery of deleted conversations or inaccessible historical attachments. Some earlier continuity work reported older records that were metadata-only. The pack is therefore a focused, evidence-led reconstruction—not a forensic export of every message ever sent.

The largest product gap is integration proof: several components have research, schemas or product rules but are not demonstrated as one released end-to-end application with tested coach and athlete surfaces.
